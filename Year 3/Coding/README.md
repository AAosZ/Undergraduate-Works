You may read the report if interested, but my collaborator and I tested Naive Bayes, Random Forest, and Multi-Class Perceptron. Ultimately, the Multi-Class Perceptron model was not only the model with one of the highest accuracies, but it was much faster at predictions than the other two models, while simultaneously (in our opinions) the easiest to implement.
